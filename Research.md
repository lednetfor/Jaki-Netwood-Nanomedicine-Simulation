Jaki-Netwood: Advanced Magnetotactic Research & Protocols1. Scientific ObjectiveThe Jaki-Netwood project explores the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Nanomedicine . Our goal is to engineer a system capable of controlling 500nm magnetotactic agents within the human body to perform targeted medical missions, such as tumor destruction and immune system priming.2. Biological Agent ProfileStrain: Magnetotactic Bacteria (MTB).Sensing Mechanism: Biogenic Magnetosomes (Fe3O4 chains).Dimensions: ~500nm.Navigation: Spiral propulsion via flagella, aligned with external magnetic fields.3. Environmental Cultivation ProtocolTo successfully cultivate and maintain these agents, the following physical and chemical constraints must be met:A. Thermal RegulationOptimal Temperature:  25°C .Reasoning: Maintains metabolic stability and prevents protein denaturation of the agent.B. Atmospheric Control (The Anoxic Requirement)Constraint: Zero to Ultra-low Oxygen environment (Microaerophilic).Degassing Process: Thermal degassing (boiling) of the medium to remove dissolved$O_2$.Purpose: Prevents the oxidation of iron, which is essential for the formation of the internal magnetic compass (Magnetosomes).C. Chemical BufferingKey Ingredient:  Calcium Bicarbonate .Role: Acts as a pH stabilizer and a carbon source. It mimics the human physiological environment, ensuring the agents are "body-ready" for future applications.4. System Architecture: The Remote Control LoopThe system operates as a closed-loop “Serious Game” simulation or real-world clinical application:The Command Center (Python): Python scripts process real-time coordinates and calculate the required magnetic vectors.The Magnetic Interface (The Scanner): A 3D array of electromagnets (MNS) translates Python logic into physical magnetic gradients.The Feedback Mechanism: Using non-invasive imaging (Ultrasound/X-ray), the position is fed back into the PC for autonomous course correction.5. Biosafety & EthicsNon-Pathogenic Design: Using strains that are inherently safe for human interaction.The Dissolve Protocol (Kill Switch): Once the target mission is achieved, the agents are programmed (via chemical or thermal triggers) to undergo bio-degradation, leaving no residue in the vascular system.6.
